The problem

Why Generic Marketing Falls Short in Your Industry

Most agencies run the same plan for every client. But a home buyer, a patient and a plant manager do not search alike. They ask different questions. They trust different sources. They also follow different rules. That gap is what an AI marketing agency by industry exists to close.

Four signs your agency does not know your industry:
  • Reports show clicks and views, not the leads your sector counts.
  • Your content reads like it could be about any business.
  • Rules that matter to you, such as health ad limits, come as a surprise.
  • Nobody can show a case study from a business like yours.

AI search by industry

AI Search Looks Different in Every Industry

AI tools do not treat every sector the same. That is the main reason an AI marketing agency by industry can do more than a generalist.

Here are three facts from outside studies.

AI Overviews on Google searches
Health searches43.0%
All topics, average20.5%
Real estate searches5.8%

Health vs. property. Ahrefs studied 146 million Google results in September 2025. AI Overviews (the AI answers at the top of Google) showed on 43.0% of health searches. They showed on only 5.8% of real estate searches. The average across all topics was 20.5%.

Software buyers
51%start with an AI chatbot
69%chose a different vendor

Software buyers. G2 surveyed 1,076 software buyers in March 2026. About half (51%) said they start research with an AI chatbot more often than with Google. And 69% said they chose a different vendor than they first planned, after an AI chatbot's guidance.

Supplier research
66%use AI to research suppliers

Supplier research. A UK survey by Magenta Associates found that 66% of senior B2B decision-makers use AI tools to research suppliers.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Industry Marketing

What is an AI marketing agency by industry?+

An AI marketing agency by industry focuses its AI search, design, web and ad work on specific sectors. It learns how your buyers search, which AI tools they ask and which rules apply to you. Then it builds one plan around that. Grozix focuses on five main industries this way and also serves others.

Why does industry focus matter for AI search?+

AI tools treat sectors differently. They show AI answers more often on health searches than on property searches. They also pull from different sources in each field. A plan built for one sector can miss the questions and sources that matter in yours. Industry focus closes that gap.

Which industries does Grozix serve?+

Our five main industries are real estate and property, healthcare and dental clinics, SaaS and technology, construction and architecture, and manufacturing and industrial suppliers. Each has its own page with our focus, method and case studies. We also serve other industries, such as ecommerce and B2B services.

What if my industry is not one of the five?+

We likely still serve it. Tell us on a strategy call. Many methods carry over, such as tracking, AI search and landing page testing. We will say which parts fit and which do not. If we have no proof in your field, we will tell you plainly before you pay.

What does AI SEO for my industry include?+

AI SEO for your industry covers the pages, facts and sources AI tools use to name you. That includes sector content, clear business details, schema (code that labels your facts) and mentions on sites your buyers trust. The mix changes by sector, so each industry page lists its own focus.

What is GEO, and how does it work in my industry?+

GEO means generative engine optimization. It helps AI tools such as ChatGPT cite your business in their answers. We study which sources those tools trust in your sector. Then we make your facts clear and easy to cite. The work differs for a clinic, a software tool and a factory.

Can I see proof from my industry?+

Yes. Each industry page links to case studies from that sector. They show the problem, what we did and the measured result. We share only real results and real client words. Visit our case studies page to filter by industry and service.

How do you handle healthcare rules and patient privacy?+

We follow Google's health ad rules and keep patient details out of your ad account. We write clear, checked content with named clinicians. We keep tracking free of private health data. Your own legal or compliance team should approve anything sensitive before launch.

Does pricing change by industry?+

No. Prices depend on scope, not on your industry. Ongoing services start from $1,000 per month. One-time projects start from $5,000. Your quote depends on your goals and the services you choose. You can see full ranges on our pricing page before you book a call.
